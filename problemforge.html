overview

Shaping unclear problems into build-ready paths.

ProblemForge is a startup where people bring real-life problems and work toward real solutions that help maintain balance in the real world.

At its core is a system for shaping unclear problems into structured workflows, clearer decisions, and build-ready execution paths: an intersection of product strategy, design systems thinking, and technical implementation.

  • problem framing and intake
  • workflow planning and execution mapping
